## Short description

A short retro browser management parody about rationing RESETs for an increasingly impatient developer community.

## Full description

You work in RESET Operations for a fictional AI coding service.

Users run out of capacity. Satisfaction falls. Frustration rises. Some leave for Klaude. Decide whether to WAIT, spend a rare Banked Reset, or authorize a GLOBAL RESET — while trying not to turn RESET spending into the company’s entire operating strategy.

Most days look like an old corporate computer terminal.

GLOBAL RESET does not.

### Features

- A compact 30-day management run, designed for roughly 5–8 minutes
- WAIT / Banked Reset / Global Reset decisions
- Satisfaction, Frustration, Expectation and user-flow management
- KROG SOCIAL MONITOR with fictional community posts
- Rare Banked Reset stock and reusable Global Reset energy
- An intentionally excessive GLOBAL RESET launch sequence
- Retro CRT visuals and optional generated electronic ambience
- Final score, Mr. Som evaluation, USER VOICES, and immediate PLAY AGAIN
- JUST RESET mode for people who do not care about responsible management

> Give me a reset, plz!  
> Give us a reset, plz!

### Controls

- Mouse or touch for normal controls
- Release Final Safety, open the Safety Cover, then hold the RESET control to 100% when prompted
- Press Space or Enter while the RESET control is focused
- Audio can be muted at any time

The Reset Company is an unofficial parody project. The company, characters, services, events and social posts shown in the game are fictionalized. The game does not retrieve or reproduce real social-media posts.
